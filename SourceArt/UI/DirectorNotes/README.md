## Storyboard_L01.png prompt

Create one finished in-game storyboard illustration for a director's clipboard clue page. Landscape 4:3 image. Monochrome hand-drawn graphite pencil, expressive but clear architectural lines, light crosshatching, warm ivory paper, generous clean margins, no words, no letters, no numbers, no checkboxes, no UI. Two stacked wide cinematic panels: upper panel shows a young woman with ponytail entering an old modest house living room through an open interior doorway, seen in three-quarter view; the room has paneled lower walls, simple cornice, a two-seat sofa in the foreground, a tall plain wardrobe near a wall. Lower panel shows the woman's silhouette in profile looking toward an odd rectangular trace of a former doorway in the paneled wall; frame the old doorway clearly but subtly, suggest the importance of what the camera can see. Small rough framing corner marks and a restrained hand-drawn oval around the odd wall detail, no directional solution arrows, no placement slots, no green/red correctness indicators. This is an atmospheric working storyboard, a visual puzzle clue, not an instruction sheet. Legible when displayed on a small held clipboard; clean silhouettes and bold focal contours, minimal background clutter. Avoid photorealistic shading or a digital vector look.

## Storyboard_L02.png prompt

Create one finished in-game storyboard illustration for a director's clipboard clue page, landscape 4:3. Match a hand-drawn graphite pencil working storyboard on warm ivory paper: strong clean focal contours, light architectural crosshatching, no words, letters, numbers, checkboxes or UI. Two stacked widescreen panels with thin imperfect pencil borders and wide clean margins. Upper panel: close view of a woman's hand holding a small unfolded architectural floor plan while she stands at a simple old-house kitchen entrance; the plan shows several simple adjacent rooms and an intriguing incomplete rectangle extending beyond a drawn wall, with one small graphite question mark only, no labels. Behind the plan, a modest dark kitchen with checkerboard floor, paneled lower walls and an old sealed rectangular doorway faintly visible on the rear plaster wall; a refrigerator and plain counter at the edges establish the kitchen. Lower panel: medium rear three-quarter silhouette of the ponytailed young actress in jacket looking at the sealed doorway trace in the paneled kitchen wall. A narrow soft wedge of light grazes the rectangular wall trace, making it readable while adjacent wall remains in graphite shadow. A small partial rough pencil oval emphasizes the trace. Do not draw a lamp placement solution, arrows, bright colors or exact object positioning. Composition teaches observation of the floor plan and light, not a mission checklist. Large simple shapes must remain readable on a held clipboard. Do not draw decorative filler, dense text, dialogue balloons or a photographed clipboard; output the flat paper artwork itself.
